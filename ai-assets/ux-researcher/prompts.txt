UX Researcher Hero Image Prompt:
Create a modern hero image for a Kuala Lumpur creative co-working space used by UX researchers. Include wireframes, sticky notes, user journey maps, usability testing boards, laptops, and a clean research-focused workspace. The image should feel professional, organized, and suitable for a UX portfolio website.

AI Tool Used:
Canva AI / Google Gemini

Purpose:
The AI image generator was used to create four different hero visuals for four visitor profiles: Portfolio, Brand Designer, Motion Graphics Artist, and UX Researcher.

Human Refinement:
The generated images were reviewed and adjusted to match the Creative Hub KL website style. The final selected images were chosen based on clarity, relevance, colour consistency, and suitability for a professional portfolio website.
